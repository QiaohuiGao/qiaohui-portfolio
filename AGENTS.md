# Portfolio design rules

These rules apply to future edits across this repository.

## Visual direction

- Preserve the original snow-mountain, forest, and river image in `assets/mountain-river-background.jpg` as the continuous site background.
- Keep the left log index fully transparent. It should contain only the site title and plain section-navigation titles—no portrait, role subtitle, status text, navigation numbering, availability card, social footer, opaque panel, border, shadow, or blur layer.
- Treat Field Notes as the editorial and professional center of the portfolio. Its large outer module must use a restrained, low-saturation gray-blue solid background that is opaque enough to prevent the global landscape from showing through.
- Individual note cards may use their own relevant article cover, diagram, or thumbnail. Keep every image strictly contained within its note card; it must never become the background of the whole Field Notes module. Prefer a separate image region and solid text region. Do not place large titles over images with strong gradient or opacity masks that make either the title or image difficult to read.
- Personal photographs may be used selectively in personal sections such as About, Experience, Projects, Skills, or Contact, while keeping the overall landscape visual language coherent.
- Prefer borderless translucent surfaces and subtle tonal separation over boxed cards, heavy outlines, or strong shadows.
- Keep typography compact and let Field Notes carry more visual emphasis than Home, About, Experience, Projects, Skills, and Contact.

## Interaction and layout

- Preserve the side navigation and smooth vertical log-style scrolling.
- Keep the content visually connected to the sidebar; do not create a wide or hard split between the two columns.
- Maintain responsive behavior, keyboard focus visibility, and reduced-motion support when changing layout or animation.
